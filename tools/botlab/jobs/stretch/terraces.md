# Terrace Heights — the Long Stages stretch (st-d)

## The cut and Δ

A hill-village stage built from nested contour regions can't be cut with a straight line: the spawn terrace's disc
reaches almost to the Largo. So the cut follows the hill.
- **Moved** by **Δ = 22 m along −Z** (`layout.js DS`):
  - the hilltop `C` and everything built round it: the spawn terrace (H4) and its H3 ring;
  - Villa Limoni, the upper funicular station, the garden wall, the back wall;
  - the Limonaia shoulder (H3 lobe, its pergola and its flight);
  - the base's props; the spawn pads, the flags, the tower goal.
- **Stays**: `C0` is where the hilltop was first drawn. The Largo, the lower half of the Scalinata, the Orto, the Case
  sul Mare lane, the headland, the crescent, the side zone and the piazza are still laid out round it. Mid is
  untouched.
- The band between the two hilltops is **new land**, drawn as new contour regions.
- The ground is regenerated by the stage's own builder and rebaked into `GROUND_DATA`: 256 rows (was 188). Its raster
  follows the back wall.
- Pure −Z, not the 17° spawn axis: a diagonal move would have pushed the funicular's upper station out past the x −28
  edge. The hill stays on its south-west wing, so the S of two hills keeps its shape; it's just longer.
- **The funicular** is now two straight reaches (`INC_SEG`):
  - A new, gentler upper reach (3.7°) runs from the moved station down to the **kink**, the point where the first
    drawing's incline came down to H2.
  - From the kink, the first drawing's own lower reach runs on to the foot.
  - The Salita beside it still steps down level by level. Its cuts are now found by track height (`incT`), so its H4
    and H3 flights spread out along the band, while its H2 and H1 flights, the Largo's west side and the tower's climb
    there stay exactly where they were.
- The stage grows 56 × 92 → 56 × 136 (bounds z ±68). The sea wall follows: the diagonal off the back moves out, and a
  new small arrow bastion is added on the promenade under the groves.

## The slice: the olive terraces and the lavatoio

The levels spread out between the spawn (H4, same height) and the Largo (H2). The Scalinata now comes down in two
flights, and the band between them is **the Oliveto**: a broad H3 olive terrace striding down from the spawn's ring to
the lower Scalinata over the Largo.
- On the Oliveto: old olives in their stone rings, the village's old olive mill (millstones on their chestnut axle,
  oil jars), and a dry-stone wall stub with an olive net rolled on a ladder.
- On its north end stands the **village lavatoio** (LAVATOIO PUBBLICO 1891): an open loggia of six limewashed pillars
  under a coppi roof, with the long double wash basin down the middle, the spring head on its low back wall, and
  washing on the line and in the basket.
- East of the Oliveto are **the lemon groves** (H2):
  - the yellow **lemon house** (LIMONAIA, the lemon trees' winter house, tall green-shuttered windows), with a vine
    pergola over lemon pots, crates and a well;
  - the groves' street, with lemon carts in turf and zones;
  - lemon trees along the groves' curved east wall.
- Below them are **the lemon walk** (H1, a pergola of lemon pots with urns at its ends) and the promenade (H0), with
  benches, a kiosk and a little lookout bastion with a telescope.
- The Limonaia above (H3, moved with the hill) sends its flight down into the groves: "the Limonaia shoulder carried
  on".
- West, the funicular's upper reach runs down the cliff with the car stopped in the band as cover, and the Salita
  (H2) steps beside it.

**Strategic point**: the lavatoio. It is 1.2 m above the lanes either side, and reachable four ways:
- from the Largo, by the lower Scalinata;
- from the groves' lane, by the Oliveto's side flight;
- from the spawn side, along the Oliveto;
- from the Salita, by its H3 flight onto the spawn's ring.

The basin, the back wall and the pillars give cover. It looks down the Scalinata to the Largo, the Vicolo and San Vito.

**Routes through the slice** (per half): three lanes and a link.
- **West**: the Salita beside the funicular.
- **Centre**: the Oliveto and the lavatoio, down the two Scalinata flights.
- **East**: the lemon groves, with the Limonaia's flight up to the spawn's ring, then the lemon walk and the promenade
  below.
- **Link**: the groves' west lane under the Oliveto's side flight ties the centre to the east lane.

**Spawn**:
- The spawn terrace keeps its exits: the Scalinata, the Salita, and the ring to the Limonaia.
- It was the stage's largest bare floor (the cover map's worst spot before), so it now has two garden urns and two
  majolica benches round the pad.
- The H3 ring below it is the defenders' apron.

**Tower Command** (`tower-data.js`): the drawn track is kept. Its last run (x −6.3 on Bravo's side) is carried on
through the groves' street to the goal, which moved out with the hill. The three checkpoints stay where drawn. The
groves' street is kept clear for it: the lemon carts are `notIn: 'tower'`, and the other props there keep off the
2.5 m lane.

## Bazookarp notes (Alpha's half; Bravo's is the mirror)

- **Checkpoint**: the lavatoio, (−9.8, −30.45, y 3.6). It's the high point between the three lanes, with four ways
  up, cover inside, and a view down to the Largo and mid.
- **Goal**: the H3 terrace at the foot of the spawn terrace's Scalinata flight, (−9.4, −44.2, y 3.6), a level below
  the spawn and 16 m from the pad. It's reached from the Oliveto (north), the Salita's H3 flight (west) and the
  Limonaia along the ring (east). Defenders see it from the spawn terrace and the flight.
- **Routes, mid → goal** (nav, Alpha's team, `route-len.js`):

  | route | via | length | swim time |

  | west | funicular foot, the Salita's flights | 72.0 m | 6.1 s |
  | groves | Orto, groves' west lane, the Oliveto's side flight | 81.7 m | 6.9 s |

  The nav's own shortest route is 53.5 m.
- **High ground**:
  - The lavatoio's and the lemon house's roofs are off-limits, like every house top.
  - The Oliveto and the Limonaia are open terraces up stairs.
  - A few jump-up tops stand in the open, so no no-carrier zone is needed: the lavatoio's back wall (1.3), the olive
    mill (1.25), urns (1.35) and lemon carts (1.25).
